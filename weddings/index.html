Weddings

Get Your Free Flower Budget Estimate

The best part of being a wedding floral designer is witnessing the reactions to the stunning display of flowers that we have created. I love the process leading up to that moment — several months, possibly even six to nine months prior. Taking a bride's idea and making it a reality truly gives me the most joy.

Since your wedding consultation may be well in advance, there are a few things you should know.

Most brides request to see the bouquets being discussed during their consultation, which is understandable. Nearly everything we purchase as consumers can be held, tried on, or tested before making a decision. Given the pressure of planning the look and feel of the most important event in your life, it makes sense to want to see the sizes, colors, and styles being considered so we can make an informed decision.

However, we often face a small challenge. Initial consultations typically occur six to nine months before the wedding. Naturally, you will wait until you have chosen your venue, dress, linens, and table settings before selecting the flowers that will tie everything together. Often, the varieties available in the studio on the day of your consultation are those in season at that time. For example, if you are meeting in February, your designer may not have peonies or Queen Anne's lace on hand, even though these blooms may be your preferred choice. You may have even chosen a particular month for your wedding because of the flowers that will be available.

With this in mind, take a deep breath and come prepared to explain your desired look for your wedding day. We will ensure to create something you will love.
